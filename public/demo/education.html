How this works

W3Schools-style: pick a topic, answer scenario-based questions, get instant feedback
1Pick a topic

Each topic has 5 questions, ordered roughly by difficulty. Beginner → intermediate → advanced. Progress badges show your best score on each.

2Answer scenarios

Each question shows a realistic dashboard reading — RSI, drawdowns, cash flow z-scores, the actual numbers you'd see — and asks what you'd do next.

3See why

After you submit, every option gets a right/wrong stamp and an explanation — the wrong ones aren't just marked wrong, they tell you the trap. Glossary links jump back to /learn for review.
