What this page is for

when the three weightings agree, the tile is the sector. when they don't, look closer.

The user-facing /commodities heatmap shows the cap-weighted 1-day return per commodity, because that's the closest analogue to how a sector ETF measures its day. Cap-weighted is the right default — it tells you what $1 in the sector did, which is the question most traders are asking.

But cap-weighted hides three legitimate questions:

  1. What did the average company do? Equal-weighted answers this. When equal disagrees with cap, the giants are pulling the sector around vs the cohort moving together.
  2. What did actively-investable shares do? Float-weighted answers this, since insider/strategic stakes don't trade. Closer to how real index funds construct, but free-float data is sparse for ASX small-caps — check floatN coverage before reading these as gospel.
  3. Is today an anomaly or a trend? Compare the 1d / 5d / 20d columns within a weighting.

Divergence callouts (|cap - equal| ≥ 1.5pp at any timeframe) highlight commodities where the cap-weighted tile materially differs from the cohort. Worth poking at the underlying constituents on /commodities/[name] before trading the tile colour.
