What this shows / example use cases
Full universe vs large+mid producers
Run the full universe first, then tighten to large+mid plus producer-stage names and compare the cluster scatter and recovery scores.
What it tells you: whether the cleaner universe sharpens commodity structure instead of washing it out with thin explorers and microcaps.
Validated packs (GMM · soft membership)
Set an exact cached filter like large+mid, producers, or heat-included, then switch method to Validated packs (GMM · soft membership).
What it tells you: the nightly probabilistic core-shell structure for that universe, including pack purity and per-name uncertainty, rather than the sharpest commodity split.
Lead-lag inside a cluster
After you find a tight commodity cluster, pick two names in section 5 and scan the cross-correlation bars across positive and negative lags.
What it tells you: whether the pair trades together contemporaneously or whether one name tends to move a few days earlier.
